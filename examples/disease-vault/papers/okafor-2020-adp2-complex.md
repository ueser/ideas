# The KRN1–ADP2 complex

Co-IP and structural work show KRN1 binds the adaptor ADP2 through its kinase domain C-lobe.
A point mutation in the C-lobe abolished binding. The KRN1–ADP2 complex is required for SIG pathway
activation in HEK293 cells: ADP2 knockout abolished SIG signaling despite intact KRN1.
