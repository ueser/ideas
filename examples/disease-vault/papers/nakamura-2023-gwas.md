# Loss-of-function KRN1 variants in nephropathy Z

Exome sequencing of 3,100 cases found rare predicted loss-of-function variants in KRN1 enriched in
nephropathy Z (OR 2.8). Carriers progressed faster. This seems at odds with KRN1 promoting
fibrosis — the authors did not examine cell types.
