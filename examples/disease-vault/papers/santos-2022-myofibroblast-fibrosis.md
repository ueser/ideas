# Myofibroblasts and renal fibrosis

Lineage tracing in mice showed that activated myofibroblasts deposit most interstitial collagen
after injury. Genetic ablation of myofibroblasts reduced renal fibrosis by ~60%.
Fibroblast contraction and ECM secretion increase with SIG signaling.
