# KRN1 kinase drives myofibroblast activation

In primary human kidney fibroblasts, siRNA knockdown of KRN1 reduced myofibroblast activation
(α-SMA, collagen I) after injury stimulus; overexpression of wild-type but not kinase-dead KRN1
enhanced it. KRN1 knockdown also lowered phosphorylation of SIG pathway effectors, and a SIG inhibitor
blocked the KRN1 overexpression effect.
