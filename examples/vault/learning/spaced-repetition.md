# Spaced repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals beats massed practice for long-term retention.
The spacing effect is one of the most replicated findings in cognitive psychology.

## How I use it
I keep flashcards for concepts I want to keep, reviewed daily. It pairs well with
[[active-recall]] since every review is a retrieval attempt.

## Caveat
Cards made from things I don't understand become rote noise. See [[elaborative-interrogation]].
