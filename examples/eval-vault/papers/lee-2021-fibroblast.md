# KRN1 kinase drives myofibroblast activation

## Results
In primary human kidney fibroblasts, siRNA knockdown of KRN1 reduced myofibroblast activation after TGF stimulation.
KRN1 knockdown also lowered phosphorylation of SIG pathway effectors.
